## What We Just Learned from Riverbrook

### Key Insights

**Riverbrook is:** Real-time operational routing (emergency brake)  
**Our system is:** Retrospective governance analysis (warning light)

**Both needed. Different purposes. Complementary relationship.**

### What We Can Adopt

1. **"Fail-Honest" Principle** (inspired by their "fail-open")
   - When unsure about ethical cost, over-report
   - Better false alarm than hidden cost
   - Add to KEYSTONE 11

2. **Graceful Degradation**
   - If moral reckoning fails, show performance metrics only
   - Never fail completely
   - Add to KEYSTONE 13

3. **Stronger Privacy Model**
   - Even more anonymous than current (if possible)
   - Learn from their anonymous dispatch pattern

## Seven Evolution Paths Identified

### Path 1: Real-World Integration
**Vision:** Close loop between operational systems and governance  
**Effort:** Medium-High (3-6 months)  
**Value:** High (realistic scenarios, early warnings)  
**Risk:** Medium (boundary erosion possible)

### Path 2: Interactive Governance Workflows ⭐ **RECOMMENDED FIRST**
**Vision:** From reports to interactive ethics committee tools  
**Effort:** Medium (2-4 months)  
**Value:** High (immediately more useful)  
**Risk:** Low (extends existing capability)

### Path 3: Temporal Self-Comparison ⭐ **RECOMMENDED SECOND**
**Vision:** Track institutional health over time (not vs others)  
**Effort:** Medium (2-3 months)  
**Value:** High (enables learning)  
**Risk:** Medium (could be misused as ranking)

### Path 4: Expanded Moral Reckoning
**Vision:** Deeper detection (predictive tensions, patterns, moral residue)  
**Effort:** High (4-6 months)  
**Value:** Very High (significantly deepens capability)  
**Risk:** Medium (complexity)

### Path 5: Multi-Institution Learning
**Vision:** Privacy-preserving collective insights  
**Effort:** Very High (6-12 months)  
**Value:** Very High (collective learning at scale)  
**Risk:** High (privacy, gaming, misinterpretation)

### Path 6: Academic Validation
**Vision:** Peer-reviewed research, published validation  
**Effort:** Very High (12-24 months)  
**Value:** Very High (credibility)  
**Risk:** Medium (results may not validate)

### Path 7: Domain Adaptations
**Vision:** Extend beyond ED (ICU, surgery, primary care, mental health)  
**Effort:** High per domain (3-6 months each)  
**Value:** Very High (broader applicability)  
**Risk:** Medium (domain-specific complexity)
